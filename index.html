Privacy Policy

NutriVibe (iOS)
Effective Date: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NutriVibe collects, uses, and protects information when you use our iOS app. We aim to keep this policy clear, honest, and easy to understand.

1. Information We Collect

NutriVibe may collect the following information to provide core app features:

  • Account Information: email address and authentication identifiers when you sign in.
  • Profile Information: information you choose to enter, such as age, sex, height, weight, goals, and preferences.
  • Nutrition and Wellness Data: food logs, water intake, fasting sessions, body measurements, recipes, and related entries.
  • Optional HealthKit Data: Apple Health data you explicitly allow (for example, weight, steps, activity).
  • Technical/Diagnostic Data: limited diagnostic information needed to maintain app stability and security.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide app functionality, including syncing your data across sessions/devices.
  • Calculate nutrition insights, goals, and progress.
  • Support account security and sign-in features.
  • Maintain reliability, troubleshoot issues, and improve app quality.
  • Enable subscription/premium entitlement features where applicable.

We do not use your information for advertising profiling.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored locally on your device and/or in secure cloud infrastructure used to provide sync features.
  • We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit.
  • We apply access controls and reasonable safeguards to protect stored data.

No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take appropriate steps to protect your information.

4. Third-Party Services

NutriVibe may use the following third-party services to operate core features:

  • Supabase for authentication and secure data storage/synchronization.
  • Apple HealthKit only if you choose to connect and grant permissions.
  • Apple In-App Purchase/StoreKit for subscription and payment processing (handled by Apple).

We do not run third-party advertising SDKs. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

NutriVibe is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without required consent.

If you believe a child has provided personal information without authorization, contact us and we will review and take appropriate action.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data, where legally applicable.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a request, contact us using the email below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product updates, legal changes, or operational needs. When we do, we will update the Effective Date above.
