Privacy Policy

NutriVibe (iOS)
Effective Date: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NutriVibe collects, uses, and protects information when you use our iOS app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: email address and authentication data.
  • Profile Information: name and user-entered details (e.g. weight, goals).
  • Nutrition and Fitness Data: food logs, calories, body metrics, and similar data.
  • Optional HealthKit Data: only if you grant permission.
  • Diagnostic Data: minimal data for stability and performance.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide core app functionality
  • Store and sync your data
  • Generate personalized insights
  • Maintain app performance and security

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

3. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on your device and in secure cloud infrastructure provided by Supabase, which we use as our backend service for authentication and data storage.

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and apply appropriate safeguards to protect your data.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase – backend and database services
  • Apple HealthKit – optional health data integration
  • Apple StoreKit – payments and subscriptions handled by Apple

We do not use third-party advertising services and do not sell your data.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Request corrections
  • Request deletion

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected with a new effective date.
